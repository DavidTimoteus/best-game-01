**Helios Bastion** is an original top-down clockwork courtyard action roguelite built with zero dependencies and pure static ES modules for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/best-game-01) "Make the best game" competition.

## Core Loop & Features
- **Sunlit Clockwork Art Direction:** Baked travertine flagstones, inlaid central brass sundial, flickering ember braziers, ink-outlined silhouettes, directional cast shadows, and volumetric sunbeams.
- **Aegis Parry-Dash & Solar Lance:** Aim and fire Solar Lance bolts (`LMB`), or dash through incoming fire (`Space` / `RMB`) to deflect enemy bolts back at 2.5x damage with hitstop and harmonic chimes.
- **5 Escalating Waves & Multi-Phase Boss:** Battle Cinder Scarabs, Prism Sentinels, Pendulum Knights, Blight Alchemists, and confront **Archon Kronos, the Eclipse Engine** on Waves 3 and 5.
- **Clockwork Relic Drafting:** After clearing each wave, choose 1 of 3 illustrated Clockwork Relics (`1`, `2`, `3` or click) to forge build synergies across Solar, Aegis, Clockwork, and Alchemist styles.
- **Zero External Requests:** Self-contained HTML5 Canvas, CSS, ES modules, and Web Audio API procedural sound synthesis.

## Controls
- **Move:** `W`, `A`, `S`, `D` or `Arrow Keys`
- **Aim & Solar Lance:** `Mouse` + `Left Click`
- **Parry-Dash:** `Space` or `Right Click`
- **Sunburst Nova:** `E` or `Q`
- **Pick Relic:** `1`, `2`, `3` or Click
- **Pause / Mute / Restart:** `P` / `M` / `R`
