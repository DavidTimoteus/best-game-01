**Prismharbor** is an original coastal lighthouse optics and tactical defense game built with zero external dependencies and pure static ES modules for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/best-game-01) competition.

## Core Mechanics & Features
- **Real-Time Lighthouse Ray Physics:** Sweep the central Solar Beacon across brass mirrors and crystal tri-splitters to reflect and refract multi-color beams across the sandstone bastion. Overlapping beams trigger exponential crossfire burn.
- **Sculpt the Mirror Lattice Mid-Battle:** Pick up and relocate portable brass mirrors (`E`), snap mirror reflection normals toward your cursor (`RMB` or `Q`), and supercharge mirrors with Lantern Flare Bolts (`LMB`).
- **Harmonic Resonance Pulse:** Press `Space` to unleash synchronized shockwaves from the Keeper and every illuminated mirror along the beam network, shattering enemy projectiles and pushing back abyssal waves.
- **7 Escalating Nights + Endless Tide:** Confront Barnacle Crawlers, Mist Stalkers, Shell Mortars, Void Sirens, **Tide Reaver** (Night 4 Boss), and **Abyssal Sovereign** (Night 7 Multi-Phase Boss).
- **Optical Blueprint Drafting:** Draft 1 of 3 illustrated Optical Blueprint relics after every cleared night (`1`, `2`, `3` or click) to forge Ruby Tri-Splitters, Chromatic Dispersion, Solar Capacitors, and Bastion Wards.

## Controls
- **Move Keeper:** `W`, `A`, `S`, `D` or `Arrow Keys`
- **Sweep Solar Beacon:** `Mouse`
- **Fire Lantern Flare Bolt (Overcharges Mirrors):** `Left Click`
- **Aim / Rotate Nearest Mirror:** `Right Click` or `Q`
- **Pick Up / Drop Portable Mirror:** `E`
- **Resonance Pulse:** `Space`
- **Draft Relic:** `1`, `2`, `3` or Click
- **Pause / Mute / Restart:** `P` / `M` / `R`
